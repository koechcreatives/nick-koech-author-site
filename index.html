About

Nick Koech is a Kenyan author, mentor, and speaker who writes across fiction, nonfiction, and screenwriting. By day, he works as a Business Development Manager at Primecap; alongside that, he's building a parallel career in publishing, mentorship, and speaking, one shaped by a belief that honest stories can help people rebuild their lives.

His work moves between registers: intimate novels about love and hardship, practical nonfiction for teachers and leaders, and a memoir drawn from his own leadership journey. What connects them is a Kenyan grounding and a habit of writing toward the reader who's in the middle of something hard.
