About

Every Story Has Something to Leave With You.

I’m Nick Koech, an author and mentor from Kenya. I write fiction, nonfiction, and screenplays, and one question sits behind all of it: what will the reader carry out of this story?

Sometimes it’s a twist you didn’t see coming. Sometimes it’s a lesson about money, ambition, or growing up that I wish someone had told me sooner. My stories draw on real life, from Nairobi streets to my roots in Kericho, and on what I’ve learned mentoring and speaking with people along the way.

Don’t just read a book. Experience a story. Discover a lesson.
